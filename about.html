A shop for Shigar, Skardu, and all of Gilgit-Baltistan

Shigari Mobile World started with one simple goal: make it easy for people in Gilgit-Baltistan to buy a phone with full confidence. In our market, customers often worry about authenticity, PTA status, and unclear pricing. We decided to build our work around honesty, quality, and long-term trust.

We proudly serve customers from Shigar, Skardu, and nearby areas — students, families, professionals, and travelers — helping them choose the right device for their budget and needs. Whether you want a flagship iPhone or Samsung, a reliable mid-range Xiaomi or Vivo, or a budget phone with strong battery, we guide you like a local friend.

What you can expect

Real guidance

We recommend what makes sense for you — camera, battery, storage, and value — not just what’s trending.

Clean deals

Clear condition, specs, and pricing. No confusing bundles or hidden surprises.

After-sale support

We help with basic setup, data transfer tips, and guidance if you face issues.

Message us your budget and preferred brand — we’ll share the best PTA-approved options available in GB.
